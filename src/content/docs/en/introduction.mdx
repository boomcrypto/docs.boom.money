Boom leverages the Stacks blockchain and sBTC to create a decentralized, non‑custodial, trust‑minimized Bitcoin payment layer for everyday use. With our PayFi-inspired approach, Boom transforms Bitcoin into a form of digital cash that’s as intuitive as sending a message—making everyday transactions simple, fast, and secure.

## Mission

Boom’s mission is to revolutionize Bitcoin payments by making them as effortless as messaging a friend. We empower individuals and businesses with financial freedom and inclusivity, ushering in a new era where Bitcoin is used like everyday digital cash.

## Vision

Our vision is to create a world where Bitcoin transactions are as seamless and routine as everyday communications. We aim to unlock global commerce and drive financial empowerment for everyone by turning Bitcoin into a practical payment tool for daily life.

## Problem

**Complexity of Traditional Systems:** Conventional financial systems are often exclusionary and overly complex, leaving many without accessible financial services.

**Everyday Usability:** Although revolutionary, Bitcoin remains difficult for the average person to use for routine transactions.

**Barriers for Small Businesses:** Outdated payment systems hinder small businesses and individuals from fully participating in the global economy.

**Lack of User-Friendly Tools:** Existing cryptocurrency wallets often overlook the ease-of-use and community engagement necessary for everyday financial interactions.


## Solution

Boom reimagines Bitcoin payments with a focus on the PayFi experience. Our smart wallet simplifies everyday transactions through:

* **Intuitive Interface & Built-In Chat:** Coordinate transactions and chat in real time—as easy as sending a text—so you stay connected while transacting.

* **Instant Payment Links & BNS Integration:** Discover and pay peers or merchants effortlessly with user-friendly payment links and the Bitcoin Naming System (BNS).

* **Multi-Token Support:** Transact with Bitcoin, sBTC, and other tokens, ensuring flexibility and choice.

* **Seamless E-Commerce Integration:** Easily integrate Boom with existing platforms, making frictionless global payments a reality.

* **Decentralized Smart Shops:** Empower merchants to create and manage secure, permissionless stores with smart contracts, embodying the full promise of PayFi.

### Implications

**Financial Inclusion:** Empower unbanked and underbanked populations worldwide with a simple, everyday digital cash solution.

**Business Empowerment:** Enable small businesses to compete globally with simplified, fee‑light transactions.

**Lower Costs & No Intermediaries:** Reduce transaction costs and eliminate unnecessary middlemen in cross-border commerce.

**Mainstream Bitcoin Adoption:** Accelerate the everyday use of Bitcoin by transforming it into a user-friendly payment method.

**Enhanced Security & Privacy:** Ensure that every transaction is secure and that users remain in full control of their keys and funds.

**Global Connectivity:** Democratize access to global markets and connect individuals and businesses seamlessly.
